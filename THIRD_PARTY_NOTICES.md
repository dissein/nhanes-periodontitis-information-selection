## NHANES public-use sources

The 2011–2012 and 2013–2014 inputs were obtained from the National Center for Health Statistics, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Public SEQN identifiers, examination weights, masked strata and PSUs support record alignment and complex-survey estimation. Source URLs and SHA256 values are supplied in `provenance/data_sources.json`.

Please cite the NHANES cycles and relevant oral-health documentation when using these records. [NHANES data and documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/)
